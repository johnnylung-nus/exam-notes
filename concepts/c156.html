K-means（K平均分群） and centroid（群中心） updates

K-means forms K groups by repeatedly assigning each point to its nearest centroid and recomputing each centroid as the mean（均值；平均數） of its assigned points. Here K is the number of clusters.

What this tells us: K means two clusters here, not two nearest neighbors. The update reaches a useful solution for this toy dataset, not a universal global-optimum guarantee.

Why do assignment and centroid updates alternate in K-means?

  • K-means alternates two operations under a squared-distance objective. With fixed centroids, nearest assignment minimizes each point’s contribution; with fixed membership, the arithmetic mean minimizes that cluster’s squared error.
  • These alternating improvements do not guarantee a global solution. Initial seeds and data geometry matter, and K must be specified. Outliers（離群值） can pull a mean, while elongated or unequal-density groups may not match the compact-centroid assumption（假設；答案依賴但題目未明說的條件）.
  • Track assignments and means separately when doing exam arithmetic. Recomputing means without reassignment is not a full iteration; evaluating a final clustering（分群） using stale centers yields the wrong error.
Implementation steps and their purpose
  1. Prepare numeric（數值型；以數量表示） features（特徵；描述樣本的屬性） and choose K
  2. Initialize K centroids
  3. Assign each point to its nearest centroid
  4. Replace each centroid with the mean of its assigned points
  5. Repeat until assignments or centroids stabilize
  6. Compare multiple initializations

Use the result and check it

Data, components and application

  • Bad initial centers → use multiple starts or K-means++ → reduce poor local solutions
  • Different scales → standardize → prevent one feature dominating
  • Outliers or noncompact shapes → inspect or choose a better grouping method → means may misrepresent the data

How to evaluate it

Compare within-cluster squared error, silhouette（輪廓係數）, stability and business profiles over plausible K values.

Common wrong answers and the exact correction
  • K-means does not require known class（類別；分類目標） labels
  • It reaches a local solution, not a guaranteed global optimum（全域最優解）.

When would this calculation be useful?

Useful for numeric data when compact groups around representative means make sense.
