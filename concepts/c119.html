Vanishing / exploding gradients（梯度爆炸）

Backpropagation（反向傳播；計算各參數梯度） repeatedly multiplies local derivatives（導數；輸入微變時輸出改變多少）. Very small products make early-layer gradients vanish; very large products make them explode, causing unstable updates.

Recognise（辨識；從資料找出物件或模式） this in a question: Loss（損失函數；衡量預測錯誤） spikes, gradients vanish/explode, deep model not learning

Why do gradients vanish or explode through repeated transformations（變換；把資料轉成另一表示）?

  • Early-weight gradients pass through chains of local derivatives. Repeated small factors produce vanishing signals; repeated large factors produce exploding signals. This is an optimization failure, distinct from fitting the training set（訓練集） too closely.
  • Saturated sigmoid（S形函數） activations have tiny derivatives when their totals are extreme. Initialization（初始化） and input scaling influence whether a deep model starts there; simply training（訓練） longer may not recover a practically absent learning signal.
  • Exploding gradients require controlled updates, such as clipping and a suitable learning rate（學習率）. Residual（殘差） paths or LSTM（Long Short-Term Memory；長短期記憶網路） address different propagation structures. Dropout is a regularizer（正則項；限制模型複雜度的懲罰）, not a universal cure for missing or enormous gradients.
Implementation steps and their purpose
  1. Inspect training loss and gradient magnitudes
  2. Check saturation, depth and initialization
  3. Choose activation-compatible initialization
  4. Use residual connections（殘差連接） for deep feedforward networks（前饋網路；訊息由前往後傳） or LSTM for long sequences（序列；有先後順序的資料）
  5. Reduce excessive learning rates and clip exploding gradients when needed

Common wrong answers and the exact correction
  • Vanishing gradients（梯度消失） and overfitting are different failures
  • Dropout is not the primary fix for exploding gradients.

Implement it and test the outcome

Data, components and application

  • Saturated sigmoid → use suitable hidden activations → avoid repeated tiny derivatives
  • Exploding gradients → clip gradient norm → cap extreme updates
  • Poor initialization → Xavier or He as appropriate → improve scale propagation

How to evaluate it

Look for stable learning and improved validation loss; a lower training loss alone does not establish better generalization.

Build the answer in this order

  1. Identify the observed failure
  2. Explain small or large derivative products
  3. Match remedy to cause
  4. Check learning and validation again

An answer you could write

  • “Repeated small derivatives weaken the correction reaching earlier weights（權重）.”
  • “I separate optimization failure from overfitting: the latter has improving training performance but worsening validation performance.”
