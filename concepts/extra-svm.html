EXTRA-SVM

Support vector machine（支援向量機）: margin（分類間隔）, scaling and business choice

A linear（線性；此處加權組合加偏置） SVM（Support Vector Machine） learns a separating boundary with a large margin. The closest influential training（訓練） cases are support vectors（支援向量；決定分類邊界的關鍵樣本）. A soft margin（軟間隔；容許違規並加懲罰） permits violations to balance fitting errors against a wider boundary.

Recognise（辨識；從資料找出物件或模式） this in a question: High accuracy（準確率） but misses rare buyers/faults, confusion matrix（混淆矩陣）, business costs

How does a maximum-margin boundary separate the classes（類別；分類目標）?

  • A linear SVM chooses a separating boundary with a large margin. Support vectors are the closest influential training cases; they define the difficult boundary region rather than every case contributing identically to its position.
  • Soft margin allows violations so noisy or overlapping data need not be perfectly separated. Larger penalty C emphasizes avoiding training violations; smaller C allows more tolerance. Tune this balance on prepared training/validation data.
  • A kernel（核函數） can provide nonlinear（非線性；不能只用線性關係表示） boundaries, but the sample explicitly compares a linear SVM with an NN. Do not claim that the given SVM already performs arbitrary nonlinear reasoning（推理）; choose between them using observed costs and metrics.

Diagnose: A feature（特徵；描述樣本的屬性）’s large units dominate the margin geometry

What you observeWhy it happensWhat to changeWhy that helps
A feature’s large units dominate the margin geometryInputs were not appropriately scaledFit a scaler on training inputs and tune C in that pipeline（處理流程）Margin geometry must reflect meaningful feature differences rather than arbitrary measurement units.
Common wrong answers and the exact correction
  • A linear SVM does not automatically model nonlinear relationships
  • The penalty strength is not a learned feature weight

Apply the distinction

Data, components and application

  • Large feature scales distort the margin → standardize → make geometry meaningful
  • Penalty too strong fits difficult/noisy cases → tune a lower penalty → allow more tolerance
  • Nonlinear structure remains → compare a kernel model if justified → permit curved boundaries

How to evaluate it

Check held-out（留出資料；未參與該階段訓練） business value, class metrics and inference（推論；由依據得出結論） cost; revisit the decision if false-positive costs are high.
