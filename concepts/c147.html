Gain ratio（增益比率） / Gini（基尼不純度）

Gain ratio adjusts information gain（資訊增益） for the number and balance of branches. Gini impurity（不純度；節點類別混雜程度） measures how mixed the class（類別；分類目標） proportions are; a good split（切分；按條件分開資料） minimizes size-weighted child impurity.

How do gain ratio and Gini compare candidate splits?

  • Raw information gain can favor many-valued attributes because splitting into many tiny groups often produces pure training leaves. A unique ID can therefore look optimal（最優） while containing no reusable rule.
  • Gain ratio divides information gain by the entropy of the branch-size distribution（分布）, accounting for how the attribute partitions the observations（觀測值）. It reduces this branching bias（偏置／偏差；須看語境） but still needs sensible candidate restrictions and pruning（剪枝）.
  • Gini impurity is one minus the sum of squared class proportions. It uses no logarithm, but its split comparison still needs size-weighted children. Greater gain is good; lower remaining Gini is good, so do not reverse the selection direction.
Implementation steps and their purpose
  1. Calculate information gain for candidate attributes
  2. For gain ratio divide by the entropy of branch sizes
  3. For Gini calculate one minus the sum of squared class proportions
  4. Weight child impurities by sample counts
  5. Choose the best valid split under the chosen criterion

Common wrong answers and the exact correction
  • Higher gain/gain ratio is preferred, but lower weighted Gini is preferred
  • Gain ratio does not eliminate every overfitting（過度擬合） risk.

Implement it and test the outcome

Data, components and application

  • Unique ID gets high information gain → remove ID or penalize branching → prevent a lookup table
  • A small unbalanced split looks attractive → enforce minimum leaf size → avoid unstable decisions

How to evaluate it

Compare pruned trees on held-out（留出資料；未參與該階段訓練） metrics; split purity alone is only a local training criterion.

Build the answer in this order

  1. Identify bias in the proposed criterion
  2. Explain the alternative in words
  3. Show impurity calculation if asked
  4. Retain validation and pruning

An answer you could write

  • “Candidate A leaves weighted Gini0.167, a reduction of0.333.”
  • “I reject a unique identifier as a generalizable purchase rule and use branching-aware criteria with pruning.”
