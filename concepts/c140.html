ROC（接收者操作特徵曲線） and gain/lift（提升度；相對於獨立情況的共現強度） charts

ROC compares true-positive rate against false-positive rate as the threshold（門檻值） changes. Gain and lift ask whether targeting the highest-scored cases finds more positives than random targeting.

Recognise this in a question: High accuracy（準確率） but misses rare buyers/faults, confusion matrix（混淆矩陣）, business costs

What this tells us: A 2.5 lift indicates useful concentration, but issuing 200 coupons is only worthwhile if the expected benefit exceeds campaign cost.

Which threshold or ranked-list view matches the business question?

  • ROC evaluates ranking across thresholds by comparing true-positive rate with false-positive rate. AUC summarizes discrimination, not the probability（機率） calibration or the profit at a particular threshold.
  • Gain asks what fraction of all positives is captured by targeting the highest-scored fraction of the population. Lift compares that capture with random targeting, so it directly relates to a limited outreach budget.
  • A ranking can have good AUC yet produce too many false positives at the selected operating point, especially with rare positives. Inspect the actual selected group and base rate rather than treating one summary score as a deployment decision.

Common wrong answers and the exact correction
  • AUC is not accuracy
  • A high AUC does not determine the best coupon threshold.

Apply the distinction

Data, components and application

  • Good overall ROC but excessive false alarms → inspect the actual threshold and precision → business workload matters
  • Evaluate training（訓練） scores → use held-out scores → avoid optimistic ranking
  • Population changes → recheck base rate and lift → targeting value can shift

How to evaluate it

Compare AUC, gain at the available budget, precision/recall（召回率） and expected profit together.

Build the answer in this order

  1. State ranking versus fixed decision
  2. Explain axes（軸；運算指定的維度） or targeted fraction
  3. Interpret the business consequence
  4. Select an operating threshold

An answer you could write
