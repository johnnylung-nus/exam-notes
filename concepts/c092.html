Basis vectors（基底向量） and energy compaction（能量集中）

A basis defines how a signal（訊號） is represented. Energy compaction seeks a few components that preserve much of the signal with low reconstruction error（重建誤差；還原值與原資料的差距）.

Input vector.

Unit-length basis vector; these vectors are mutually perpendicular.

Projection coefficient, obtained by the dot product.

Reconstruction from the retained components.

With all orthonormal basis vectors reconstruction is exact; retaining fewer components gives an approximation.

  1. w1=(1+2)/√2=3/√2; w2=(−1+2)/√2=1/√2.
  2. w1u1=(1.5,1.5); w2u2=(−.5,.5).
  3. Both sum to(1,2).
  4. First-only residual（殘差）=(−.5,.5); squared error=.5.

Interpret the result: Two coefficients reconstruct exactly; one coefficient incurs squared error.5.

What this tells us: The first-only representation saves one coefficient but loses a specific difference between the original coordinates. Whether that difference matters depends on the target problem.

Why can a few basis coefficients preserve most signal energy?

  • A coordinate value depends on the chosen basis. Changing basis can concentrate useful variation into fewer coefficients without changing the underlying signal when all components are retained.
  • For an orthonormal basis, dot（內積；逐項相乘後相加） products give the component weights; reconstruction adds each basis vector scaled by its corresponding weight.
  • Energy compaction seeks directions that reduce total reconstruction error. It does not directly optimize recommendation（推薦） accuracy（準確率） or a particular class（類別；分類目標） boundary.
  • Dropping components is the compression step; the task must tolerate the discarded information, including potentially small but important anomalies.
Implementation steps and their purpose
  1. Prepare comparable signal vectors.
  2. Choose or learn basis directions.
  3. Project each signal to obtain component weights.
  4. Retain the useful components.
  5. Reconstruct using their weighted sum.
  6. Compare reconstruction loss（損失函數；衡量預測錯誤） and downstream（後續階段；使用前一步結果的模型或流程） usefulness.

Use the result and check it

Data, components and application

  • Too few directions → keep more components → preserves important variation.
  • Unrepresentative training（訓練） signals → learn on representative data → basis may otherwise fit the wrong patterns.
  • Low-variance but important event lost → evaluate the target task → energy retained is not identical to task relevance（相關性；是否切合問題）.

How to evaluate it

Check reconstruction squared error, size/computation savings and classification（分類；預測類別） or detection（偵測） quality.

Build the answer in this order

  1. Need compact representation.
  2. Basis and projection.
  3. Retain components and reconstruct.
  4. Measure information loss and task value.

Common wrong answers and the exact correction
  • A component weight is not the basis vector.
  • Energy compaction is not the recommendation problem solved by MF（Matrix Factorization；矩陣分解）.

When would this calculation be useful?

Use when raw sensor vectors are large or redundant（冗餘；資訊重複） and a compact representation is needed.
