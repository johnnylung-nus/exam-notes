KG（Knowledge Graph；知識圖譜） traversal（遍歷） and multi-hop（多跳） queries

Multi-hop queries follow several relationships. Conjunctive（合取；各條件都要成立） queries require all stated conditions, often joining or intersecting results.

Recognise（辨識；從資料找出物件或模式） this in a question: Shared vocabulary, type constraints（約束條件）, relationship queries, multi-hop facts（事實；推理中已知的資訊）

What this tells us: Returning B or C because each satisfies one branch answers OR, not AND. Drug C may be relevant（相關性；是否切合問題） in reality, but a missing protein path prevents a source-backed assertion（斷言；明確提出的知識陳述） from this graph.

How does a question become a path through a graph?

  • A path query composes relationships; a conjunctive query joins several conditions on the same candidate. The difference matters: drugs reaching an Alzheimer-linked protein form one candidate set, while drugs in a trial form another; the answer to AND is their intersection, not their union.
  • The sequence（序列；有先後順序的資料） and direction of edges determine what the path means. “Disease associated with Protein targeted by Drug” can require following a relation backwards from protein to drug. Natural-language word order alone is not enough to determine traversal direction; inspect the stored schema（知識結構）.
  • Traversal cannot use a missing link even if that relationship is real. This is why a graph-completion model may be proposed to suggest candidates, but predicted edges should not silently become established query facts. An answer can be precise over known links while having poor recall（召回率） because the graph is incomplete.
Implementation steps and their purpose
  1. Identify the anchor entity（實體；被描述的對象） and desired answer type.
  2. Write the sequence of relation directions.
  3. Traverse the relevant links.
  4. For AND conditions, retain only entities meeting every branch.
  5. Return the answer and supporting paths.

Choose the method from the problem

Useful for questions such as finding a team owner through its player, or finding entities satisfying several connected criteria.

Common wrong answers and the exact correction
  • AND is not a union of unrelated answers.
  • Predicted edges require validation before being treated as facts.

Implement it and test the outcome

Data, components and application

  • Graph incompleteness → label（標籤；作比較的正確答案） incomplete coverage or propose verified link completion → traversal cannot follow missing edges.
  • Unbounded traversal → constrain relation types/depth → avoid exploring irrelevant paths.

How to evaluate it

Check each hop, direction and join. Measure correctness and coverage separately; an empty result may mean（均值；平均數） insufficient data.

Build the answer in this order

  1. Parse target and anchors.
  2. Draw required path/branches.
  3. Apply all conditions.
  4. Explain missing-data limitations.

An answer you could write

  • I first follow the Alzheimer→associated protein←targets drug path, then retain only those same drugs with a Phase II trial relation.
  • Drug B fails the trial condition and Drug C lacks a known protein path; an AND answer must satisfy both branches on one drug identifier.
