Case-Based Representation

Store past problems together with their solutions/outcomes as cases. The representation is the case record; case-based reasoning retrieves, adapts and checks earlier experience for a new problem.

What to draw / write: Case-based representation

Case #101
Symptoms → Diagnosis → Treatment
Case ID
Identifies the earlier case.
Problem description
Its observations/symptoms and relevant context.
Solution/outcome
What was concluded/done and, if recorded, how it worked.

Exam answer: Write a case record with fields that support comparison and reuse. Show how the new case retrieves a similar case, how its solution is adapted and how the outcome is checked. The slide gives the three-part chain; extra fields should be justified by the scenario.

Used Cases:

The use-case names come from the lecture. The short scenarios below explain how to recognize them in an exam; they are teaching examples, not quoted exam questions.

Lecture use caseWhat it meansExample / clue in a question
Diagnostic systems（診斷系統）Use previous successful/failed cases to help solve a current problem.Find a past equipment fault with similar observations, adapt its solution, and check whether it works for this case.
Product design（產品設計）Reuse and adapt relevant earlier designs.Retrieve a similar product design and modify it for a different size or operating requirement.
Decision support（決策支援）Present relevant experience to help a person choose.Show similar project cases, their decisions and outcomes; explain differences before recommending a choice.

Choose by the required job: explain what knowledge is stored and how this representation helps with the scenario. A use-case name alone is not a sufficient reason.
