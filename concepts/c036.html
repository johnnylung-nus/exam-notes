C036

Association-rule（關聯規則；哪些項目常一起出現） confidence（信賴度；前件出現時後件也出現的比例） and lift（提升度；相對於獨立情況的共現強度）

Confidence asks how often the consequent（後件；THEN結果） occurs when the condition occurs. Lift compares that confidence with how common the consequent already is.

Recognise（辨識；從資料找出物件或模式） this in a question: Products bought together, baskets, support（支持度；同時出現的交易比例）, confidence, diagnostic associations

Conditional confidence

confidence(A→B)=count(A∩B)count(A)lift(A→B)=confidence(A→B)support(B)
A, B
Antecedent and consequent itemsets.
count(A)
Transactions containing the antecedent.
count(A ∩ B)
Transactions containing both itemsets.
support(B)
Fraction of all transactions containing B.
lift
Observed co-occurrence relative to independence; association is not causation.
  1. home appears in sessions1,3,4,5,6,7:6 triggers.
  2. news appears in1,2,4,6,7:5 triggers.
  3. Both occur in1,4,6,7:4 joint baskets.

Interpret the result: home→news=4/6; news→home=4/5. Confidence depends on direction.

Illustrative directional confidence and lift

Worked study example: Use 100 baskets: A appears 20 times, B 40 times, both 12 times. Lift is study elaboration; support/confidence are explicitly（明確；直接提供的資訊） taught in inspected slides.

What this tells us: A 60% confidence is meaningful only alongside its baseline, support and application role. In another dataset where B occurs in 80% of all baskets, 60% confidence would be worse than that baseline.

What do confidence and baseline frequency tell us?

  • Confidence measures the conditional success rate among baskets where the antecedent occurs.
  • It is directional: the same joint count divided by different condition counts yields different confidence for A→B and B→A.
  • This is why frequent-itemset discovery alone does not determine the best rule direction.
  • A popular consequent can make confidence look strong without any useful association.
  • Lift compares conditional frequency with the consequent’s overall frequency.
  • Above one means positive association relative to that baseline; one means no improvement in this comparison.
  • It still does not establish a cause or a safe clinical action.
  • Filtering（濾波） must also respect the intended prediction（預測） flow.
  • Even a high-support, high-confidence rule that predicts another treatment instead of current diagnosis（診斷；判斷可能的疾病或故障原因） does not answer the sample diagnostic question.
  • Item-role labels（標籤；作比較的正確答案） make this filtering explicit; a mining algorithm cannot infer（推論；由依據得出結論） past/current roles from an unordered basket.
Implementation steps and their purpose
  1. Count baskets with the condition.
  2. Count baskets with both sides.
  3. Confidence = both count / condition count.
  4. For lift, divide confidence by overall consequent frequency.
  5. Apply supplied thresholds and domain constraints（約束條件）.

Data, components and application

  • Popular consequent → compare baseline/lift → high confidence can be uninformative.
  • Small condition count → require support and validate → confidence from a few examples is unstable.

How to evaluate it

Check rule direction, counts and holdout（留出資料；未參與該階段訓練） behaviour. Lift above one suggests positive association, not causality or clinical validity（推論有效性）.

Build the answer in this order

  1. Calculate support/confidence.
  2. Check baseline prevalence.
  3. Apply thresholds.
  4. Explain relevance（相關性；是否切合問題） and limitations.

Where this is tested in the sample papers

ISY5001 Q3(b)(i) — Temporal（時間的） association baskets and preprocessing（前處理）

What the question requires

  1. Choose the basket unit.
  2. Join patient records and order by date.
  3. Encode earlier diagnosis/treatment, time gap and current diagnosis with distinct roles.
  4. State basket count and assumptions（假設；答案依賴但題目未明說的條件）.

Proposed answer points

  • One consultation per basket: prior（先驗機率；看證據之前） diagnoses + prior treatments + time since each treatment + current doctor diagnosis.
  • Assuming the original 5,000 consultation records and visit date as a treatment-date proxy, create about 5,000 baskets. New trial consultations are not quantified.

Avoid: Patient count does not determine consultation-basket count. Time since treatment differs from duration of treatment. IDs join records; they are not normally mined items.

ISY5001 Q3(b)(ii) — Filter mined rules for the diagnostic task

What the question requires

  1. Keep a current-diagnosis consequent.
  2. Use antecedents available before diagnosis.
  3. Apply justified support/confidence thresholds.
  4. Check temporal meaning and clinical plausibility.

Proposed answer points

  • Role-labelled items let a filter reject treatment-only consequents from a diagnosis ruleset（規則集；一組可使用的規則）.
  • Case-derived illustration: prior lens replacement + elapsed time（已經過的時間；本次與過往事件的時間差） at least six months → current PCO（Posterior Capsule Opacification；後囊混濁） diagnosis association. This is not a clinical rule to apply without validation.
  • With 100 baskets, 20 containing the antecedent and 12 containing both: support=12%; confidence=60%. Pass/fail depends on chosen minima.

Avoid: Ordinary baskets have no time order: encode roles/time. Association does not prove causation or safe treatment.
