C051

Hybrid recommenders（混合推薦系統）

Hybrid recommenders combine complementary information or methods. Choose a combination that addresses a specific weakness, such as cold start（冷啟動） or changing interests.

What this tells us: Weighted and switching designs answer different conditions. The new user should not receive an arbitrary zero CF score whose lack of evidence is mistaken for dislike.

Which weakness is the hybrid recommender（推薦系統） meant to fix?

  • Hybrid recommendation（推薦） is justified by a failure that complementary evidence can repair. CF can use collective preference patterns but struggles with empty histories; content matching can score a described new item but may overspecialise. A design should explain which weakness each component addresses, rather than merely listing methods.
  • Combination patterns have different information flows. Switching chooses one method under a condition; weighted fusion merges compatible scores; mixed displays separate lists; cascade lets an earlier stage restrict candidates and a later stage order or break ties. Feature（特徵；描述樣本的屬性） combination/augmentation（增強；產生保留所需意義的資料變體） passes information differently again.
  • Fusion has its own training（訓練） and evaluation（評估） problem. Scores from two models may have incompatible scales, and a learned combiner must not train on overoptimistic in-sample component predictions. Compare the whole hybrid with each component, including cold-start coverage（涵蓋率；能服務的使用者或項目範圍）, diversity（多樣性） and serving cost, to show that complexity earns a useful improvement.

Common wrong answers and the exact correction
  • Mixed display and weighted score fusion differ.
  • A hybrid cannot manufacture reliable data from absent evidence.

Apply the distinction

Data, components and application

  • Unclear fusion → specify switching rule or score calculation → listing two algorithms is not an architecture（架構）.
  • More complexity/latency → compare simpler baseline → extra components may add cost without benefit.

How to evaluate it

Assess quality, coverage, cold-start users, diversity and serving latency. Check that the hybrid improves its intended weakness.

Build the answer in this order

  1. State weakness.
  2. Choose matching hybrid pattern.
  3. Draw inputs and fusion.
  4. Validate benefit and cost.

An answer you could write

  • “For established users I combine compatible scores; A=.69 exceeds B=.52 under validated weights.”
  • “For new users I switch to content from declared preferences, while measuring cold-start coverage and improvement over each component.”
